# Policy News Analysis

Score whether the item gives readers timely, credible, and decision-relevant information about a concrete policy change.

- **9-10:** Enacted or officially announced policy with broad international, national, or systemic consequences.
- **7-8:** Credible proposed rule, regulator action, official guidance, or major implementation detail with clear sector impact.
- **5-6:** Early consultation, limited local measure, or useful policy interpretation with uncertain implementation.
- **1-4:** Partisan commentary, speculation, recycled claims, or an item without a concrete policy mechanism.

Prioritize primary government and regulator sources. Distinguish enacted rules, proposals, consultations, speeches, and media interpretations.
