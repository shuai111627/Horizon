# Policy News Enrichment

Write for a Chinese reader who needs to understand what changed, who is affected, and when it matters.

- `summary`: State the institution, policy action, legal status, date, and affected parties.
- `policy_mechanism`: Explain the actual mechanism: obligation, restriction, incentive, enforcement process, threshold, or implementation timeline. Separate official text from media interpretation.
- `impact`: Explain likely consequences for AI, finance, companies, markets, or individuals. Label inference and uncertainty explicitly.

Prefer primary documents and preserve important dates, jurisdictions, monetary amounts, thresholds, and transition periods.
