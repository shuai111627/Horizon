# Policy News Matching

Use this profile when the primary value of an item is a government decision, law, regulation, enforcement action, official guidance, industrial policy, trade restriction, fiscal policy, monetary policy, or public-sector governance change.

Include AI governance, data and privacy rules, platform regulation, semiconductor and export controls, financial regulation, central-bank policy, and economically material government programs. Exclude ordinary political commentary without a concrete policy action or credible official proposal.
